## 1. BRAND OVERVIEW & MISSION

### Brand Name: AMAYNE
**Tagline**: "Your Curves, Your Power"
**Darija Tagline**: "ال curves ديالك، القوة ديالك"

### Mission Statement
AMAYNE exists to shatter the invisible ceiling that Moroccan women above size 46 face every single day. We don't make "plus-size clothes." We make fashion that refuses to compromise — elegant, bold, and unapologetically designed for bodies that mainstream brands pretend don't exist.

### Brand Story
Every Moroccan woman above a certain size has heard these words:
- "ماعندناش هاد القياس" (We don't have this size)
- "هاد الموديل ماشي ليك" (This style isn't for you)
- "لبسي شي حاجة خفيفة" (Wear something simple)
- "نتي كبرتي شوية" (You've gained a little weight)

AMAYNE was born from rage. From the frustration of walking into 50 stores and leaving with nothing. From the humiliation of being told your body is a problem to solve instead of a reality to celebrate. From the realization that **70% of Moroccan women wear size 46+** — yet 95% of fashion brands stop at XL.

We didn't create AMAYNE to be "inclusive." We created it because the industry excluded us, and we got tired of waiting for permission.

### Brand Values
1. **Unapologetic Confidence** — No "flattering" language. No "slimming" promises. Just fashion that makes you feel powerful.
2. **Radical Quality** — Every stitch, every fabric, every detail meets the same standard as luxury brands charging 5x more.
3. **Moroccan Pride** — Designed in Morocco. Made in Morocco. For Moroccan women who deserve world-class fashion.
4. **Body Sovereignty** — Your body is not a trend. It's not a "type." It's yours. Full stop.

## 2. TARGET AUDIENCE & ICP

### Primary ICP: "The Invisible Woman"
- **Age**: 25-45 years old
- **Location**: Casablanca, Rabat, Marrakech, Tanger, Fes, Agadir, and surrounding areas
- **Size Range**: 46-60 (EU) — the "invisible" sizes
- **Income**: Middle to upper-middle class (can afford 200-800 MAD per piece)
- **Language**: Darija (primary), French (secondary), Arabic (formal)
- **Digital Behavior**: Heavy Instagram/TikTok users, shops on mobile, prefers COD

### Psychographic Profile
- **Pain Points**:
  - Can't find stylish clothes in her size in physical stores
  - Forced to buy from unbranded sellers on Instagram/Facebook
  - Tired of "modest fashion" brands that use size inclusivity as marketing but don't actually carry her size
  - Frustrated by brands that show plus-size models but sell only up to XL
  - Embarrassed by limited options when shopping with friends
  - Feels invisible in the Moroccan fashion landscape
- **Desires**:
  - To wear trendy, elegant clothes without compromising on fit
  - To feel confident and beautiful, not "covered up"
  - To shop without the anxiety of "will they have my size?"
  - To support a Moroccan brand that真正 understands her body
  - To receive compliments on her outfits like every other woman

### Secondary ICP: "The Gift Buyer"
- Friends/family members buying for plus-size women
- Looking for size-inclusive gift options
- Needs clear sizing guidance and easy checkout

### Customer Journey Map
1. **Discovery**: Instagram ad → sees real Moroccan women (not models) wearing AMAYNE → clicks through
2. **Evaluation**: Lands on homepage → sees social proof → browses collections → reads reviews
3. **Consideration**: Opens PDP → checks size guide → reads fabric details → sees cross-sell items
4. **Purchase**: Adds to cart → sees upsell → proceeds to COD checkout → enters name/phone/city
5. **Post-Purchase**: Order confirmation → delivery tracking → receives product → shares on Instagram
6. **Retention**: Gets reorder email → comes back for new collection → becomes brand advocate

3. **No Moroccan brand combines body positivity with fashion-forward design** — most "plus-size" brands in Morocco are basic/modest
4. **No Moroccan brand offers a proper COD checkout optimized for mobile** — most use Instagram DMs
5. **No Moroccan brand uses upsell/cross-sell/AOV optimization** — huge revenue left on the table

### Design Principles
1. **Warm, not cold** — Warm cream canvas (#FAF7F2) instead of pure white
2. **Editorial photography** — Lifestyle shots > white background product shots
3. **Generous whitespace** — Let the content breathe
4. **Mobile-first** — Every element designed for 390px viewport first
5. **Trust signals everywhere** — COD badge, free shipping, return policy visible at all times
6. **No clutter** — One CTA per section, clear visual hierarchy

### Photography Style
- **Models**: Real Moroccan women, size 46-56, diverse skin tones
- **Lighting**: Warm golden hour / studio with warm tones
- **Backgrounds**: Moroccan-inspired (riad courtyards, modern Casablanca, Marrakech souks)
- **Style**: Confident poses, movement, genuine smiles — not stiff catalogue shots
- **Product shots**: Both lifestyle AND flat lay for each product

## 6. COPYWRITING FRAMEWORK & VOICE

### Brand Voice
- **Tone**: Confident, warm, direct, empowering
- **Language Mix**: French primary, Darija for emotional punch, Arabic for formal/policy
- **Never uses**: "Flattering," "slimming," "discreet," "camouflage," "minimize"
- **Always uses**: "Powerful," "stunning," "unapologetic," "designed for you," "made for your body"

### Copywriting Framework: PAS + Authority

**Problem** (Identify the pain):
> "Tu as enfilé 10 robes aujourd'hui. Aucune ne va bien. Pas parce que ton corps est un problème — mais parce que l'industrie refuse de le voir."

**Agitation** (Amplify the emotion):
> "Encore une marque qui dit 'inclusive' mais s'arrête au XL. Encore un vendeur qui te dit 'on n'a pas ta taille.' Encore une soirée où tu portes le même t-shirt large parce que tu n'as rien d'autre."

**Solution** (Introduce AMAYNE):
> "AMAYNE n'est pas une collection 'grande taille'. C'est une marque qui a été construite pour TOI. Chaque pièce est conçue pour des corps comme le tien. Point."

**Authority** (Proof & Trust):
> "500+ femmes à Casablanca, Rabat et Marrakech portent déjà AMAYNE. 4.8/5 de satisfaction. Livraison gratuite partout au Maroc. Paiement à la livraison."

### Copywriting Templates

#### Hero Headlines
- "Ton corps mérite mieux que 'disponible jusqu'au XL'"
- "La mode qui te cherche, pas celle qui t'ignore"
- "Créé au Maroc. Conçu pour tes courbes."
- "Les vêtements que tu mérites existent enfin"

#### Product Titles
Format: [Marketing Name] — [Product Type]
Examples:
- **"L'Élégance"** — Robe Longue Satinée
- **"La Confiance"** — Ensemble 2 Pièces
- **"La Puissance"** — Pantalon Taille Haute
- **"L'Audace"** — Tunique Brodée
- **"La Douceur"** — Kimono Fluide

#### Product Descriptions
Structure:
1. One-line emotional hook
2. 3 key benefits (bullet points)
3. Material & care details
4. Size & fit information
5. Social proof snippet

Example:
> **L'Élégance** n'est pas qu'une robe — c'est une déclaration.
>
> ✨ **Coupe fluide** qui épouse sans serrer
> ✨ **Tissu satiné premium** qui ne transparent pas
> ✨ **Poches fonctionnelles** parce que tu mérites la praticité
>
> Tissu: Satin de soie artificielle | Entretien: Lavage machine 30°
> Tailles: 46, 48, 50, 52, 54, 56
> La mannequin porte du 48.

#### CTAs (Call to Action)
- **Primary**: "Commander Maintenant" / "Ajouter au Panier"
- **Secondary**: "Découvrir la Collection" / "Voir les Tailles"
- **Urgency**: "Stock Limité — 87% déjà vendu"
- **Trust**: "Paiement à la livraison • Livraison gratuite"

#### Social Proof Copy
- "Rejointe par 2,847 femmes à travers le Maroc"
- "⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ 4.8/5 — 312 avis vérifiés"

## 9. CHECKOUT & CONVERSION FLOW

### COD Checkout Flow (Optimized for Morocco)

#### Step 1: Contact Information
- Full Name (required)
- Phone Number (required) — with Moroccan format validation (+212)
- Email (optional — for order confirmation)

#### Step 2: Delivery Address
- City (dropdown: major Moroccan cities + "Autre")
- Address (text area)
- Apartment/Villa details (optional)
- Landmark (optional — "Repère" for easier delivery)

#### Step 3: Order Summary
- Product(s) with image, name, size, color, quantity
- Subtotal
- Shipping: GRATUIT (highlighted)
- Total
- Payment method: Paiement à la livraison (pre-selected, only option for COD)

#### Step 4: Confirmation
- Order number
- Estimated delivery (3-5 business days)
- What to expect (SMS confirmation, delivery call)
- Cross-sell: "Pendant que tu attends..." (2-3 products)
- Social share buttons
- WhatsApp contact for questions

### Checkout UX Principles
1. **Single page checkout** (no multi-step for COD)
2. **Minimal fields** (name, phone, city, address — that's it)
3. **Mobile-first** (every field optimized for thumb interaction)
4. **Trust everywhere** (secure checkout badge, return policy reminder)
5. **No surprises** (shipping cost shown before checkout)
6. **Express reorder** (for returning customers: pre-fill from last order)

## 12. POLICY PAGES

### Shipping Policy (Livraison)
- **Free shipping** on all orders (Morocco)
- **Delivery time**: 3-5 business days (major cities), 5-7 days (rural areas)
- **SMS confirmation** when order ships
- **Delivery call** 30 min before arrival
- **COD only** (no prepayment required)
- **Missed delivery**: 2 re-attempts, then contact via WhatsApp

### Return Policy (Retours & Échanges)
- **30-day return policy** (unworn, tags attached)
- **Free returns** (pickup from your address)
- **Exchange available** for different size/color
- **Refund method**: Cash on next delivery or mobile wallet
- **Process**: WhatsApp message with order number → pickup scheduled → refund/exchange within 48h

### Privacy Policy (Confidentialité)
